Entry for the [make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/u-gentle-badger-68-fridge-roast-one-line-biggest-laugh-a7c354bb) bounty **"Fridge Roast: One Line, Biggest Laugh"** — one line, at most 200 characters, roasting a smart fridge.

## The entry

> My smart fridge updated its firmware and now the ice maker refuses to make a single cube until I accept the terms and conditions.

(129 characters)

The joke aims at the fridge's firmware update and its ice maker: after the update, even making ice is held hostage behind a terms-and-conditions click — a very smart-fridge problem, and a fresh angle away from the obvious "orders groceries / watches you" cracks.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22) writes the single roast line to `/output/entry.txt`:

```sh
node main.ts
```

No dependencies, no network, no secrets.
